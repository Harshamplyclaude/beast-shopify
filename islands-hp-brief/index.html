Islands × Harry Potter · Hoodie brief · Drop 01

Beneath the Trapdoor

Five hoodies for the Islands Harry Potter drop, each designed the way a homegrown streetwear label would do it. The collection follows the protections guarding the Philosopher's Stone, which is how Season 1 of HBO's series ends. The series premieres on 25 December 2026.

Prepared 31 Oct 2026For: founders, design, influencer teamLenses: Bluorng · Almost Gods · Deadbear · Jaywalking

The rule: three distances

Souled Store sells proof that you're a fan. Islands should sell proof that you're in on it. Every hoodie has to work at three distances, and the gap between them is what we film.

5 m

Streetwear first

Silhouette, wash and texture carry it. From across the street it's simply a great hoodie. No faces, no logos, no quote across the chest.

50 cm

The reference

Up close, a fan catches it and the conversation starts. Each piece has one story the wearer can repeat in a sentence when someone asks "what's that?"

5 cm

The hidden line

An inside label, a pocket print, a neck tape that only the owner knows about. This layer powers "details you missed" posts after the drop.

Why the trapdoor

It's the season everyone will be watching. HBO's first season is Philosopher's Stone, 8 episodes, premiering on Christmas Day. Its climax is the run through the protections under the trapdoor: Fluffy, Devil's Snare, the flying keys, the chessboard, the troll, the potions and the Mirror of Erised.

Everyone knows the scenes, but nobody has made clothes from them. All of these are in the first film, so the references land with casual fans. Mass merch never goes past crests, Platform 9¾ and the glasses. That gap is our differentiator.

It gives every piece a reason to exist. Each hoodie is one protection. Ask "why this design?" and the answer is never "it looked cool". The Stone sits at the bottom, so it is the last and hardest piece to get.

It turns into an Islands game. Protections are levels. A new level opens on Islands each week of the season, so people have a reason to come back every week the show is on air. Details below.

01

Out of Bounds

Lens: DeadbearFluffy, the three-headed dog · Drop A

What the wearer says when asked"It's Fluffy. He only falls asleep when there's music on."

5 mFirst look
A washed cocoa, heavyweight hoodie with a chunky, fuzzy character on the chest. It reads as a cute-ugly mascot piece.
50 cmThe reason
It's Fluffy: three heads, all asleep, each in headphones. In the story Fluffy guards the trapdoor and only sleeps when music plays. Headphones on, world off is the most relatable Gen Z mood, told through a Year One secret.
Back: THIRD-FLOOR CORRIDOR / OUT OF BOUNDS in stencil, with a small trapdoor.
5 cmHidden
Printed inside the pocket bag: "I shouldn't have said that." Hagrid's line after he lets slip how to get past Fluffy.
Build
420 GSM garment-dyed fleece, oversized. Chenille appliqué heads, so the dog is actually fluffy to touch. Puff-print headphones, printed pocket bag. Colourways: washed cocoa, heather grey.
Lens
Going by its listings, Deadbear does heavyweight 420 GSM oversized basics with character graphics. Fluffy is the story's ready-made mascot. Commission an original Fluffy illustration and don't borrow anything from Deadbear's own characters.
The ad "Guarded. Unless there's music."

A POV reel: the creator is mid-chaos, puts headphones on, and the shot cuts to the hoodie. It works for music, college and everyday creators, so it's cheap to seed widely.

Licensing: new Fluffy artwork (headphones added) needs licensor approval. Submit it first.

02

Stop Fighting It

Lens: JaywalkingDevil's Snare · Drop B

What the wearer says when asked"It's Devil's Snare. Hit it with a flash and it glows, because light is what beats it."

5 mFirst look
A boxy, cropped black hoodie wrapped in hand-drawn vines. They climb from the hem, cross the seams, wind round the sleeves and onto the hood. It looks like an artist's piece.
50 cmThe reason
It's Devil's Snare. Harry and Ron fight it and it grips tighter; Hermione tells them to relax, and it lets go. "The more you fight, the tighter it gets" is printed small on the lower back. For an anxious generation, that is a line worth wearing every day.
5 cmHidden
Parts of the vines and the words LUMOS SOLEM are printed in reflective ink. In daylight you can't see them; under a phone flash they flare silver, because light is what beats the Snare. Inside hem label: "Sulks in the sun."
Build
Boxy, cropped, gender-fluid fit. Engineered placement print so each vine lines up across panels. A few raw-edge appliqué tendrils left half-attached. Reflective silver ink. Colourways: washed black, bottle green.
Lens
Jaywalking works from its founder's own artwork, with deconstructed, boxy, gender-fluid shapes. Here, one drawing covers the whole garment and ignores the seams.
The ad "Turn your flash on."

A night shoot. With the flash off it's a moody black hoodie. Flash on, and the vines and LUMOS SOLEM light up. Flash reveals already do well for reflective streetwear, and this one has a story behind it.

Licensing: no characters. Check that the spell name and the Snare's look are on the licensed asset list.

03

Broken Wing

Lens: BluorngThe flying keys · Drop A

What the wearer says when asked"They're not birds, they're keys. Find the one with the broken wing."

5 mFirst look
A washed powder-blue hoodie with a quiet flock of birds crossing the chest and flying over one shoulder. Calm and wearable, an everyday piece.
50 cmThe reason
They're keys with wings. One key, in antique brass, has a bent wing: the one Harry catches. He spots it because a Seeker notices what everyone else misses, and the hoodie asks the viewer to do the same.
5 cmHidden
Embroidered inside the left cuff: "Big. Old-fashioned. Bent wing."
Build
Heavyweight brushed fleece, oversized. Around 20 tonal embroidered keys across the front, shoulder and back, with one in brass metallic thread. Colourways: washed powder blue, faded ecru.
Lens
Bluorng keeps garments quiet, with muted washes, tonal detail and nature motifs like birds. A flock of birds is exactly the graphic Bluorng would make. These happen to be keys.
The ad "Only a Seeker finds it in three seconds."

A "find the key" challenge: the creator turns and the video freezes for three seconds. The comments become the game ("found it at 0:04"), and every rewatch helps the reel's reach.

Licensing: original artwork with no characters. Low risk.

07

Mirror

Hero for adsLens: BluorngThe Mirror of Erised · Drop A

What the wearer says when asked"You can only read it in a mirror, like the Mirror of Erised."

5 mFirst look
A sun-faded bone hoodie with raised, tone-on-tone lettering in a thin arched frame. Clean and textural. The text looks like a code.
50 cmThe reason
The lettering is mirror-written: I SHOW NOT YOUR FACE BUT YOUR HEART'S DESIRE. It only reads in a mirror, like the Mirror of Erised, where Harry saw the family he never had. Every mirror selfie becomes the reveal.
Back: the mirror's carved inscription, ERISED STRA EHRU OYT UBE CAFRU OYT ON WOHSI, arched across the shoulders.
5 cmHidden
Printed neck tape: "Men have wasted away before it." Dumbledore's warning about the mirror.
Build
400–450 GSM washed fleece, boxy oversized, no drawcords. High-density tonal puff print, fine tonal embroidery for the frame. Colourways: sun-faded bone, washed slate. Test the reveal with real mirror selfies on iPhone and Android before sign-off.
Lens
Tone-on-tone raised print is how Bluorng adds detail without adding noise. Here the tonal print hides the trick until a mirror shows it.
Why hero
The product makes its own content: every buyer's mirror selfie works as an ad, and no explanation is needed. It is also the easiest piece to produce in time for the premiere.
The ad · two formats "This hoodie only makes sense in a mirror."

Mirror reveal: a normal shot where nobody can read it, then flip to the mirror and it reads. Street "Muggle test": ask strangers to read the hoodie, then hold up a mirror. UGC prompt for the comments: "What would the mirror show you?"

Licensing: text only, no likeness. The lowest risk in the set.

∞

The Stone

Numbered · raffle onlyLens: Almost GodsThe Philosopher's Stone · Season finale

What the wearer says when asked"It's the Philosopher's Stone. You can't buy it, you have to win it."

5 mFirst look
A washed black hoodie, almost bare, with one blood-red stone at the centre of a fine line diagram. The stone catches the light.
50 cmThe reason
The stone sits inside the "squared circle", the old alchemists' symbol for the Philosopher's Stone. 665 on the back is Nicolas Flamel's age in Year One. VAULT 713 on the cuff is the Gringotts vault Hagrid collects it from. It's the object everyone in Year One is after, and it waits at the bottom of the trapdoor.
5 cmHidden
Hood lining printed with an alchemical manuscript page. Numbered inner label, for example No. 041 / 100.
Build
Washed black heavyweight fleece, double-layer hood, no drawcords. Hand-beaded or zardozi stone, tonal discharge diagram, blackletter print on the back. Small numbered run.
Lens
Almost Gods builds on the line "paleolithic emotions, medieval institutions, and godlike technology", with bursts of handwork against quiet space. The Stone is medieval alchemy that grants near-godlike power. If Islands goes after one real collab, this is the pitch to take to them.
The ad "665. If you know, you know."

A macro craft film of the stone being stitched by hand, ending on: "You can't buy this one. Get through the trapdoor." It works as the halo piece that sends people to the Islands game.

Licensing: the squared circle is public-domain alchemy. Confirm that the 665 and Vault 713 details are covered by the license scope.

What goes around the hoodies

The letter (packaging)

Every order arrives as a Hogwarts-style letter addressed in emerald-green ink, with the buyer's real name and city and a line like "Sorry this is late. The owls rarely fly to Indore."

Inside: the hoodie's one-sentence story, plus a hint about where its 5 cm detail is. This audience waited for that letter at eleven. The unboxing hook writes itself: "My Hogwarts letter finally came."

The trapdoor game (on Islands)

Each week of the season a new level opens on Islands: find the key, pick the right potion, finish a chess endgame. Clearing a level earns raffle tickets for The Stone.

Everyone who clears every level gets early access to Drop B, so losing the raffle still pays off. SNKRS showed that losing every draw drives people away for good.

What we won't make

  • Actor faces or film stills. Likeness rights are a separate approval, and they're the clearest sign of mass merch.
  • Quotes across the chest: "Always", "Mischief Managed", "I solemnly swear".
  • Flat-printed house crests, the Deathly Hallows sign, Platform 9¾, glasses and scar.
  • Gold foil, glitter, a big Hogwarts crest on black.
  • A mass graphic on a 400 GSM blank. A heavy blank doesn't make a cheap idea premium.

Kith's licensed capsules (Marvel, Looney Tunes) and Almost Gods × FILA work for the same reason: the IP sits inside the label's own design system. You see the label first and the IP second.

Licensing checks before design starts

  • Scope: film assets, book text, Wizarding World lore, HBO series assets. Some details here come only from the books (the potion riddle, the inside-label lines).
  • Every new artwork goes through licensor approval, usually at concept, sample and production stage. That time has to be in the calendar.
  • No actor likeness anywhere. None of these concepts need it.
  • The four brands are a design method. Don't use their names, marks or signature graphics in product or ads unless a collab is signed.

Lens notes

What's public about each label, and what we borrowed. The team knows these brands better than the press does, so correct anything that's off.

Bluorng

Delhi, 2020. Minimal and refined; the garment itself is the design work, and the brand controls its visual world tightly. Borrowed: tonal detail, muted washes, nature motifs.

Almost Gods

Delhi, 2018, founded by Dhruv Khurana. Myth, history and Indian handwork; "maximalist minimalism", bursts of energy with quiet pockets. The FILA collab was built on worldbuilding. Borrowed: one handwork burst on a quiet garment.

Jaywalking

Mumbai, 2019, founded by Jay Jajal. Deconstruction and experimentation, the founder's own artwork, boxy and gender-fluid silhouettes. Borrowed: one drawing over the whole garment, seams ignored.

Deadbear

New Delhi, stocked by Crepdog Crew. Little has been written about it; its listings show heavyweight 420 GSM oversized basics and character graphics. Borrowed: the heavyweight blank and a mascot. Worth checking with the team.
