# Policies to paste (Settings > Policies)

The Shopify connection can't edit policies (it lacks `write_legal_policies`),
so paste these in by hand.

## Refund policy

### Size exchanges
Wrong size? You can exchange any item for a different size within 30 days of
delivery. It must be unworn, unwashed and have its tags attached. Email
mrbeast@amplybrands.com with your order number and the size you need, and we
will arrange a pickup from your address.

### Damaged, faulty or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, faulty, or is not what you ordered, tell us within
30 days of delivery and we will replace it. A continuous unboxing video showing
the sealed parcel, the shipping label and the item is required for these claims.

### Refunds
We do not accept change-of-mind returns. If the size or design you need for an
exchange or replacement is out of stock, we refund that item to your original
payment method.

### What cannot be exchanged
- Items reported more than 30 days after delivery.
- Items that have been worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.
- Items sent back without their tags.
- Items bought from a seller we have not authorised.

10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. IST.

## Shipping policy

### Dispatch
Orders are packed and handed to our courier within 1-2 business days. Pre-order
items ship on the date shown on their product page.

### Delivery
We ship across India. Delivery typically takes 3-7 days after dispatch.

### Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders of Rs 999 and above. Orders below Rs 999 have a flat
Rs 100 delivery charge, shown at checkout before you pay.

### Payment
We accept prepaid orders only. Cash on delivery is not available.

### Tracking
You will receive a shipping confirmation with tracking details once your parcel
leaves us. If tracking does not move for a few working days, email
mrbeast@amplybrands.com with your order number.
